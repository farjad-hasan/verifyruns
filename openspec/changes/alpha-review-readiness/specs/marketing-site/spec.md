## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Positioning describes the actual verification scope
Landing, setup, pricing and product documentation SHALL describe independent destination count and field checks, missing-run detection and reported failures. They SHALL disclose that aggregate checks do not verify record identities or arbitrary values, and SHALL NOT claim no competitor reads destinations or guarantee unmeasured setup times.

#### Scenario: Prospective reviewer
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the landing page
- **THEN** they can identify append-only syncs as the initial use case and reach an explanation of limitations before signing up

### Requirement: Planned features are not advertised as available
Pricing SHALL distinguish free early-access capabilities from proposed paid packages. Unimplemented client grouping, branding and priority delivery SHALL NOT appear as currently available. Price-interest actions SHALL retain the chosen plan through signup and clearly state no payment is taken.

#### Scenario: Agency interest
- **WHEN** a visitor reads pricing
- **THEN** they see what works today separately from future commercial plans and are not promised agency management
